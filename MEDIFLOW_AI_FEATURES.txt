                    Next-Generation Emergency Healthcare System

MediFlow AI is an intelligent healthcare coordination platform designed to bridge 
patients, emergency responders, and hospital command centers in real time. It 
streamlines triage, ambulance dispatch, facility capacity matching, and emergency 
admissions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MULTI-ROLE AUTHENTICATION & ACCESS CONTROL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• 3 Tailored User Roles:
  - Patient / Citizen: Emergency assistance, hospital discovery, ambulance tracking.
  - Hospital Staff / Doctor / Nurse: Command center, live triage queue, fleet management.
  - System Administrator: Platform oversight, analytics, and hospital onboarding.
• Sign Up & Account Creation:
  - Register as Patient, Hospital Staff, or Administrator.
  - Capture hospital affiliation, credentials, license IDs, and contact info.
  - Persistent account storage across user sessions.
• Seamless Role Switching & User Profiles:
  - Edit personal health records (Blood group, allergies, chronic conditions, emergency contacts).
  - Quick role toggling for testing and administrative previews.

2. AI-POWERED EMERGENCY ASSESSMENT & SMART TRIAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Interactive Triage Assessment:
  - Step-by-step clinical questionnaire assessing chief complaints, vitals, pain level.
  - Emergency severity score calculation (Immediate, Urgent, Less Urgent, Routine).
• Automated Pre-Arrival Clinical Summary:
  - AI-generated clinical handover report sent ahead of time to the receiving ER.
  - Highlights red-flag symptoms (chest pain, stroke signs, severe trauma).
• Immediate Action & First-Aid Guidance:
  - Step-by-step first-aid protocols displayed while awaiting transport or arriving at ER.
